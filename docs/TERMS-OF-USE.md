This is the plain-text copy of the terms page bundled inside every PDF.

**You may**

- Print and use these pages with your own students or children.
- Use them in your classroom, co-op, therapy room or home.
- Share a printed copy with a parent of a child you teach.

**Please do not**

- Share the digital file or post it on a website or shared drive.
- Resell, redistribute or claim the artwork as your own.
- Use the pages to create another product for sale.

Extra licences for teammates are available at a discount.

## What you own, and what you can sell

The doodle artwork in this repository was drawn from scratch for this project
(plain SVG paths in `ispy/icons/`), so there is no third-party clipart licence
to worry about and nothing to attribute.

The two typefaces are used under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, which allows
commercial use:

- **Fredoka One** - `assets/fonts/OFL-FredokaOne.txt`
- **Amatic SC** - `assets/fonts/OFL-AmaticSC.txt`

All text on the pages is converted to vector outlines at build time, so the
font files themselves are never embedded in or redistributed with the PDFs.
The OFL does not restrict documents that merely *use* a font, so the finished
printables are yours to sell.
